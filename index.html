Pace

A companion for the graduated return-to-activity plan your clinician gave you.

Pace does not diagnose concussions, and does not decide when you are ready to progress. It helps you follow the plan your own clinician has already given you, and log symptoms worth discussing at your next appointment. Always follow your clinician's guidance over anything shown here. If your symptoms are worsening, seek medical attention.

Your graduated return-to-activity stages

The published framework's minimum is 24 hours symptom-free before progressing. If your clinician asked for longer, set it here — Pace will hold you to the longer window. It will not let you set anything shorter than the framework minimum.
